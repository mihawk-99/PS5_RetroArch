# PS5 RetroArch v0.2.0-alpha.1

Native RetroArch for jailbroken PS5 consoles, rendering through
[PS5_Vulkan](https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_Vulkan). This release adds GPU-rendered
GameCube/Wii and PlayStation 2 emulation, and fixes every crash testers reported
on earlier builds.

> **Alpha.** The games and paths below are the ones I have tested on my console.
> Other games may still fail. Please report them (see *Reporting problems*).

## Highlights

- **Eight cores.** New since v0.1.0-alpha.1: **Dolphin** (GameCube and Wii),
  **LRPS2** (PlayStation 2) and **PPSSPP** (PSP). All three render on the PS5's GPU
  through PS5_Vulkan. They join FCEUmm, mGBA, Snes9x, FinalBurn Neo and Genesis Plus GX.
- **PlayStation 2 at 4K.** LRPS2 uses its Vulkan hardware renderer by default at
  6x internal resolution, with multi-threaded VU1. The God of War II and
  Final Fantasy X demos and GTA San Andreas run at full speed on my console.
  **You need your own PS2 BIOS** in `system/pcsx2/bios/`.
- **GameCube and Wii.** Dolphin 2609 with its JIT and fast memory. Wind Waker,
  Resident Evil 4, Super Smash Bros. Melee, Mario Kart Wii and Rogue Leader are tested.
  Wind Waker ran for an hour and RE4 for 30 minutes with no crash.
- **PSP.** PPSSPP v1.20.4 on its Vulkan renderer with JIT: God of War: Ghost of
  Sparta and Yu-Gi-Oh! GX Tag Force are tested at 10× internal resolution.
- **120 Hz output** where the display supports it, with the correct 60 Hz fallback.
- **Faster save states.** A 50 MB PS2 save state is written without stdio, which
  was the slow part.

## Fixed from tester reports

| Report | Status in this release |
| --- | --- |
| Crash after closing a game (seen with an SNES game) | **Fixed.** The video driver was being re-created on a RetroArch thread, and the console gives such threads only 64 KiB of stack. It crashed with *Threaded Video* on. RetroArch's threads now get 2 MiB, and the stack-heavy code moved to the heap. I reproduced the crash, then confirmed the fix on the console. |
| PSP game (Ratchet & Clank: Size Matters) crashing after one to two minutes | **Fixed** in the GPU driver: its internal lists were changed from several threads at once without a lock. A new driver test reproduces the corruption on the old driver and passes on the new one. PPSSPP ran a 10-minute soak with the menu opened 25 times and did not crash. |
| Every game at **half speed with V-Sync on** | **Fixed.** On a display that only refreshes at 60 Hz, the console accepted the 120 Hz mode and kept refreshing at 60 Hz, so each frame was shown twice. The driver now measures the real refresh rate and falls back to 60 Hz. My display is 120 Hz, so the 60 Hz path still needs confirming on a 60 Hz TV. |
| Rogue Leader crash after about three minutes | **Fixed** (a race in Dolphin's dual-core GPU FIFO). |

With the half-speed fix, **V-Sync on** is the recommended setting on every display.

## Stability work in this release

- Every thread the title, RetroArch and the driver start now asks for an adequate
  stack instead of the console's 64 KiB default.
- Driver state that several threads can reach is atomic or locked. A debug-table
  counter could write past its array when two threads recorded commands at once.
- Before release, every core was run with a game: boot, menu open and close, then a
  Quick Menu action, with Threaded Video on for half of them and V-Sync on
  throughout. There were no crashes and no fatal signals.

## Known issues

- **Shader compilation stutter.** The first time a game needs a new shader, it can
  hitch while the shader compiles; later runs load it from the cache. Dolphin's
  default is still *Synchronous* compilation, and the driver compiles one pipeline
  at a time. Asynchronous compilation is the next piece of work.
- **Rogue Leader** runs at 72–85% for stretches of its attract sequence, even with
  every shader cached. The cost is the driver's CPU work, and it is being worked on.
- **PAL PS2 games** (50 fps) on a 120 Hz output alternate two and three refreshes per
  frame, which can look uneven. The console offers no 50/100 Hz mode.
- **PS2 games in `.7z` archives** are not unpacked. Use ISO, CHD, CSO or another
  supported disc format.
- Mario Party (Wii): black flicker on the world map was reported and has not been
  investigated yet.

## Install or update

1. Copy the complete `PPSA99169` folder to your homebrew title location, for
   example `/data/homebrew/PPSA99169/`, and launch it from your homebrew launcher.
2. Updating keeps your `config/`, `savefiles/`, `savestates/`, `system/` and
   `content/`. Settings already saved there, such as a core's internal resolution,
   are kept rather than replaced by new defaults.
3. Put games in `content/` and BIOS files in `system/` (PS2: `system/pcsx2/bios/`).
   No games or BIOS files are included.

## Reporting problems

Include the core, game, file format and the settings you changed. After the
problem, and **before relaunching**, copy `trace.txt` and `retroarch.log` from the
title folder: a new launch replaces them.
