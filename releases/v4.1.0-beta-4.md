## PS5 RetroArch is a passion project, not a piracy project.

It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

<a id="starting"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> RetroArch starts every time

Some launches closed straight back to the home screen, most often when EmulationStation started a game or RetroArch was opened again soon after closing. Until the console restarted, the next launch could fail too.

- **The cause:** at startup RetroArch asks the bundled Lapy daemon for its permissions. It checked its access while that request was still being answered, and Lapy, seeing the title busy, refused it.
- **The fix:** RetroArch now waits until the request is granted before it checks anything.
- **On a Base PS5:** 10 launches and 5 quick restarts in a row, every request granted. Before, the third launch after a restart was refused.

<a id="smb"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/cloud.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> SMB

- **No freeze when you open Load Content → SMB.** RetroArch could stop answering while it waited on the share; it now never waits on the network longer than its time limit.
- **Saving works on a fresh install.** The WebUI's SMB page could test a server but not save it until RetroArch had made its settings folder. It now makes the folder itself.
- **.7z archives load over SMB.** RetroArch opens the archive on the share and unpacks the game from it, as it does for local files. The archive stays compressed on your PC.

<a id="library"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/library.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Your library

- **Games whose names begin like a BIOS file are listed again.** The library hid any file whose name started with a BIOS name of any core, so a game could vanish because of another system's BIOS. It now ignores only files that match the BIOS names of that system's own cores, as a whole name.

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from v4.0.0-beta-4

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content and any custom media.
2. **Use the WebUI's Update button**, or merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the package into your installation, keeping your own files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch. If a launch was refused before the update, restart the console once.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
