## PS5 RetroArch is a passion project, not a piracy project.

It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

<a id="store"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-square-down.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Store updates

- **Each release now raises its content version.** Store apps such as ProsperoStore compare the content version of your installed copy with the newest release's, and offer an update only when the release's is higher. Every release until now reported `01.000.000`, so none was ever offered.
- **This release reports `04.003.000`**, higher than every earlier release, so a store can offer it to every installation. Each release from now on raises it again, and the release build refuses one that does not.

<a id="screenscraper"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/image.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> ScreenScraper

- **A search waits its turn.** With a one-thread ScreenScraper account, searching for a game on the scraping page while a scrape job was fetching another sent two requests at once, one over the account's limit. The search now waits until the current game is finished.

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from v4.2.0-beta-4

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content and any custom media.
2. **Use the WebUI's Update button**, or merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the package into your installation, keeping your own files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
